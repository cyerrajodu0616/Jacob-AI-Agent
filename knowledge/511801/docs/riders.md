# Riders — NewBridge Final Expense (511801)

## Included rider (no additional cost)

- **Accelerated Death Benefit Rider (ADBR) for Terminal Illness** — included at
  no additional cost on every policy, with both the Level and the Modified death
  benefit. It lets the insured access part of the death benefit early if they
  are diagnosed with a qualifying terminal illness.

## Optional rider (additional cost)

- **Accidental Death Benefit (AD) rider** — optional, for an additional premium,
  and must be elected at the time of application (at issue). It pays an
  additional benefit if the insured dies as the result of an accident.
  - Available only with the **Level** death benefit — it is not offered on the
    Modified death benefit.
  - Available for issue ages 50–80.
  - Rider benefit amount: $2,000–$35,000, matching the base policy's coverage
    amount.
  - Coverage and premiums for the rider end at age 100.

The AD rider's premium depends on the applicant's age; the exact amount comes
from the eApp's quote screen.

## Not currently offered

- A Child/Grandchild rider is **not currently available** on this product.
