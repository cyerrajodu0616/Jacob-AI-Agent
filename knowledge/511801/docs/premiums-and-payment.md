# Premiums and Payment — NewBridge Final Expense (511801)

## Payment methods

- Bank draft (ACH from a checking or savings account).
- Debit and credit cards.
- If a draft fails, the payment method defaults to direct bill.

## Who can pay (payor)

The payor can be the applicant, the applicant's spouse, or the applicant's
domestic partner. If the payor is someone other than the applicant, the eApp
collects that person's details (name, contact, and banking information).

## Payment frequency (modes)

Premiums can be paid monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually. Premiums
are paid until age 100.

## Draft date options

- The first draft can be scheduled for a future date, up to one month from the
  application date.
- A specific day of the month, from the 1st through the 28th.
- A specific day within a specific week of the month.
- Alignment with the applicant's Social Security payment date.

## Changing coverage after issue

- Coverage increases are not allowed. To add coverage, the applicant purchases
  additional coverage rather than increasing the existing policy.
- Coverage decreases are allowed, down to the policy's minimum coverage amount.

## Policy fee

There is a $25 annual policy fee, applied according to the payment frequency.
