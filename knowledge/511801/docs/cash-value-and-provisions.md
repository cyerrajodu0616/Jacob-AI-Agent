# Cash Value, Loans and Policy Provisions — NewBridge Final Expense (511801)

As a whole life policy, this product builds cash value over time. The following
provisions apply.

## Loans and withdrawals

- Policy loans are available against the policy's cash value.
- The loan interest rate is fixed at 8% (the maximum allowed).
- Maximum loan amount: the net cash value, less any due and unpaid premiums.
- Minimum loan: $500. Minimum payment: $25.

## Surrenders

If the policy is surrendered, upon termination the policyholder receives the
cash surrender value — or, if applicable, a refund of unearned premiums
(including any rider premiums).

## Grace period

The policy has a 31-day grace period for premium payments.

## What happens if the policy lapses (missed premiums, start to finish)

The published chain when premiums go unpaid:

1. A missed premium starts the 31-day grace period.
2. A failed bank draft also switches the payment method to direct bill.
3. If the client stops paying entirely and the policy lapses, the automatic
   non-forfeiture option is Extended Term Insurance (ETI); taking the cash
   surrender value instead is the optional alternative.
4. A lapsed policy can be reinstated with a statement of good health.

## Cash value and non-forfeiture

- The policy accumulates cash value at the statutory maximum non-forfeiture
  interest rate.
- There is no automatic premium loan option — premiums are not automatically
  paid from the cash value.
- If the client stops paying premiums entirely (premiums stop), the automatic
  non-forfeiture option is Extended Term Insurance (ETI); taking the cash
  surrender value instead is available as an optional alternative.
