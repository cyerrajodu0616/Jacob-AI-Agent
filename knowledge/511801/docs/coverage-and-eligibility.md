# Coverage Amounts and Eligibility — NewBridge Final Expense

## Coverage range

- Minimum coverage: $2,000
- Maximum coverage: $35,000
- Coverage is selected in $1,000 increments.
- Default starting quote amount: $2,000.

## Maximum coverage by age and rate class

The maximum available coverage depends on the applicant's age and the rate
class assigned:

- **Under age 75**: Level Preferred $35,000 · Level Non-Tobacco $35,000 ·
  Level Tobacco $35,000 · Modified Non-Tobacco $20,000 · Modified Tobacco
  $20,000
- **Age 75**: Level Preferred $20,000 · Level Non-Tobacco $15,000 · Level
  Tobacco $15,000 · Modified Non-Tobacco $10,000 · Modified Tobacco $10,000
- **Ages 76–80**: Level Preferred $20,000 · Level Non-Tobacco $15,000 · Level
  Tobacco $15,000 · Modified Non-Tobacco $10,000 (Modified Tobacco is not
  available in this age band)
- **Ages 81–85**: Level Preferred $20,000 · Level Non-Tobacco $15,000 (only
  Level Preferred and Level Non-Tobacco are available in this age band)

## Issue ages

Issue ages are based on age last birthday and depend on the death benefit type
and tobacco use:

- **Level death benefit** — Non-Tobacco: ages 50–85. Tobacco: ages 50–80.
- **Modified death benefit** — Non-Tobacco: ages 50–80. Tobacco: ages 50–75.

Premiums are paid until age 100; the policy matures at age 121.

## Eligibility basics

- The policy owner must be the insured.
- The application asks whether the applicant is a US citizen or legal permanent
  resident.

## State availability

- The product is available in 47 states.
- It is NOT available in California, Florida, New York, or South Carolina.
- Every other state is available. Availability follows the applicant's state of
  residence (the quote screen fills the state from the residence ZIP code and
  blocks ZIPs in states where the product isn't offered).
