# Product Overview — NewBridge Final Expense

## What this product is

Product type: whole life insurance — simplified issue, with an instant
underwriting decision (not term insurance).

NewBridge Final Expense is a simplified-issue, instant-decision whole life
insurance product. It is designed to help cover end-of-life expenses such as
funeral costs, probate fees, medical bills, and other final debts.

The carrier is Continental General.

## Death benefit options

The product offers two death benefit types:

- **Level death benefit** — the full death benefit is payable in all policy
  years.
- **Modified death benefit** — a graded death benefit in the early years (this
  may vary by state):
  - Year 1: 110% of premiums paid (including any rider premium, less outstanding
    debt).
  - Year 2: 120% of premiums paid (including any rider premium, less outstanding
    debt).
  - Year 3 and later: 100% of the face amount.
  - If death is the result of an accident, the full face amount is paid from day
    one.

Which benefit type applies is determined by underwriting — agents quote it but
do not choose the final class.

## Who can be covered

- Gender: male or female.
- The policy owner must be the same person as the insured.
- Acceptable beneficiary relationships: spouse, domestic partner, child,
  parent, and sibling.

Issue ages are based on age last birthday and depend on the death benefit type
and tobacco use:

- **Level death benefit** — Non-Tobacco: ages 50–85. Tobacco: ages 50–80.
- **Modified death benefit** — Non-Tobacco: ages 50–80. Tobacco: ages 50–75.

## Key product rules — premiums and maturity

- Premiums can be paid monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually, and are
  paid until age 100. The policy matures at age 121.

## Key product rules — replacements, reinstatement and conversion

- Replacements and 1035 exchanges are not permitted.
- Policy reinstatement is allowed with a statement of good health.
- No conversion privilege — the policy cannot be converted to another product.

## Key product rules — grace, contestability and misstatement

- Grace period: 31 days.
- Standard contestability terms apply.
- Misstatement of age or gender: if the insured dies and the date of birth or
  gender on the application was wrong, the payable amount is the benefit that
  the last premium paid would have bought at the correct age/gender.

## Carrier

The carrier — the insurance company that issues and backs the policy — is
Continental General. (NewBridge Final Expense is the product name, not the
name of the insurer.)
